## 分析

US real PCE rose 0.4% in June while the PCE price index fell 0.1% month-on-month; nominal income growth slowed to 0.2%.

### 直观解读

The combination is more consistent with real spending holding up despite slower nominal income growth because the monthly PCE price index fell; it does not establish a durable consumption acceleration from one month.

### 变化

- Nominal PCE grew 0.3%, down from 0.9% in May, while real PCE held at 0.4%.
- Headline PCE prices fell 0.1% month-on-month after a 0.5% May increase; core PCE prices rose 0.1% after 0.3%.
- BEA revised April and May estimates.

### 有限推论

- Inference: June reduced short-run price pressure in this measure while real consumption remained positive.

### 限制与口径

- PCE price and CPI are distinct measures.
- April and May were revised in this release.

### 其他可能解释

- Monthly price movements and revised source data can reverse in subsequent releases.
- The aggregate cannot identify which household income groups changed spending.

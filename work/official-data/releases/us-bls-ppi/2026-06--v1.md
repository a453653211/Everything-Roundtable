## 分析

U.S. final-demand PPI fell 0.3% in June, but the decline was concentrated in energy and goods while services and the core measure still rose.

### 直观解读

The combination is more consistent with a sharp easing in volatile goods and energy pricing than with broad producer-price deflation: gasoline and energy pulled the headline down, while service prices and the core measure continued to rise.

### 变化

- Final-demand PPI shifted from +0.6% in May to -0.3% in June, seasonally adjusted.
- Final-demand goods fell 1.4%, led by a 6.4% energy decline; final-demand services rose 0.2%.
- The measure excluding foods, energy, and trade services slowed from +0.8% in May to +0.1% in June; its 12-month increase was 5.1%.

### 有限推论

- Inference: headline pipeline price pressure eased in June, but persistent services and core increases limit the evidence for broad disinflation.

### 限制与口径

- The monthly figure is seasonally adjusted, while the 12-month figure is unadjusted.
- This is the first local baseline for this dataset; exact February-May revision deltas were not reconstructed without prior local records.

### 其他可能解释

- Energy-price volatility can reverse quickly and may dominate the one-month headline.
- Monthly revisions and the June industry resampling can affect comparisons for detailed components.

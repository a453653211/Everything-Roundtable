## 分析

US real GDP grew at a 1.5% annualised rate in Q2, slower than Q1, while private domestic final sales accelerated to 3.9%.

### 直观解读

The combination is more consistent with private domestic spending remaining firmer than the GDP headline than with a broad domestic-demand collapse, because final sales to private domestic purchasers accelerated even as government spending, investment and exports reduced headline momentum.

### 变化

- Headline real GDP slowed from 2.1% SAAR in Q1 to 1.5% in Q2.
- Consumer spending, investment and exports increased, while government spending decreased; imports also increased.
- The domestic-purchases price index accelerated to 5.7% from 3.6%.

### 有限推论

- Inference: the softer headline does not by itself show a comparable weakening of private domestic demand.

### 限制与口径

- Quarterly growth is annualised and not directly comparable to month-on-month or year-on-year rates.
- This is an advance estimate.

### 其他可能解释

- The advance estimate will be revised as source data are completed.
- Imports subtract from the GDP accounting identity and do not on their own signify weaker domestic demand.
